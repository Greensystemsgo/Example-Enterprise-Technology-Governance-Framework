# Risk Register Template

## Risk Register Fields

### Basic Information
- **Risk ID:** Unique identifier for the risk (e.g., RISK-2024-001)
- **Risk Title:** Brief descriptive title of the risk
- **Risk Description:** Detailed description of the risk and its potential impact
- **Risk Category:** Category of risk (e.g., Security, Operational, Financial, Compliance, Technical)
- **Risk Owner:** Person or team responsible for managing the risk
- **Date Identified:** Date when the risk was first identified
- **Date Last Updated:** Date when the risk was last updated

### Risk Assessment
- **Likelihood:** Likelihood of risk occurrence (1-5 scale)
- **Impact:** Impact if risk occurs (1-5 scale)
- **Risk Score:** Calculated risk score (Likelihood × Impact)
- **Risk Level:** Risk level based on score (Low, Medium, High, Very High)
- **Risk Status:** Current status (Open, Mitigated, Accepted, Transferred, Avoided)

### Risk Management
- **Mitigation Strategy:** Description of mitigation strategies and controls
- **Mitigation Owner:** Person or team responsible for implementing mitigations
- **Mitigation Status:** Status of mitigation implementation (Not Started, In Progress, Completed)
- **Residual Risk:** Remaining risk after mitigation implementation
- **Risk Acceptance:** Whether residual risk is accepted and by whom

### Monitoring and Review
- **Review Date:** Next scheduled review date
- **Review Frequency:** How often the risk should be reviewed
- **Key Risk Indicators:** Indicators that signal increased risk likelihood or impact
- **Monitoring Method:** How the risk is monitored and tracked
- **Escalation Criteria:** Criteria for escalating the risk to higher authority

### Related Information
- **Linked Exception ID:** Reference to related security exceptions or waivers
- **Related Risks:** References to related or dependent risks
- **Business Impact:** Description of potential business impact
- **Regulatory Impact:** Description of potential regulatory or compliance impact
- **Financial Impact:** Description of potential financial impact

## Risk Categories

### Security Risks
- **Cybersecurity Threats:** Malware, phishing, ransomware, insider threats
- **Data Breaches:** Unauthorized access to sensitive data
- **System Vulnerabilities:** Unpatched systems, misconfigurations, weak controls
- **Identity and Access Management:** Unauthorized access, privilege escalation
- **Physical Security:** Unauthorized physical access to facilities or equipment

### Operational Risks
- **System Availability:** System downtime, service interruptions
- **Performance Issues:** Slow response times, capacity constraints
- **Data Loss:** Data corruption, accidental deletion, backup failures
- **Process Failures:** Inadequate procedures, human error
- **Vendor Dependencies:** Vendor failures, service disruptions

### Financial Risks
- **Budget Overruns:** Project cost overruns, unexpected expenses
- **Vendor Costs:** Unexpected vendor costs, contract disputes
- **Compliance Fines:** Regulatory fines, penalties
- **Business Impact:** Revenue loss, productivity loss
- **Investment Loss:** Failed projects, wasted resources

### Compliance Risks
- **Regulatory Violations:** Non-compliance with regulations
- **Audit Findings:** Audit deficiencies, control gaps
- **Policy Violations:** Non-compliance with organizational policies
- **Legal Issues:** Legal disputes, liability exposure
- **Reputation Damage:** Public relations issues, stakeholder concerns

### Technical Risks
- **Technology Obsolescence:** Outdated technology, compatibility issues
- **Integration Failures:** System integration problems, data migration issues
- **Scalability Issues:** Performance problems, capacity limitations
- **Maintenance Challenges:** Difficult maintenance, high support costs
- **Innovation Risks:** New technology adoption, implementation challenges

## Risk Assessment Scales

### Likelihood Scale (1-5)
- **1 - Very Low:** Event is very unlikely to occur (0-10% probability)
- **2 - Low:** Event is unlikely to occur (10-25% probability)
- **3 - Medium:** Event is possible to occur (25-50% probability)
- **4 - High:** Event is likely to occur (50-75% probability)
- **5 - Very High:** Event is very likely to occur (75-100% probability)

### Impact Scale (1-5)
- **1 - Very Low:** Minimal impact to business operations
- **2 - Low:** Limited impact to business operations
- **3 - Medium:** Moderate impact to business operations
- **4 - High:** Major impact to business operations
- **5 - Very High:** Critical impact to business operations

### Risk Level Classification
- **Low Risk (1-4):** Standard monitoring and management
- **Medium Risk (5-9):** Enhanced monitoring and management
- **High Risk (10-16):** Intensive monitoring and management
- **Very High Risk (17-25):** Critical monitoring and management

## Risk Management Strategies

### Risk Mitigation
- **Preventive Controls:** Controls that prevent risk occurrence
- **Detective Controls:** Controls that detect risk occurrence
- **Corrective Controls:** Controls that correct risk occurrence
- **Compensating Controls:** Alternative controls when primary controls are not feasible

### Risk Acceptance
- **Acceptable Risk:** Risk level that is acceptable to the organization
- **Risk Tolerance:** Maximum acceptable risk level
- **Risk Appetite:** Desired risk level for the organization
- **Risk Acceptance Criteria:** Criteria for accepting residual risk

### Risk Transfer
- **Insurance:** Transfer risk to insurance provider
- **Contracts:** Transfer risk through contractual agreements
- **Outsourcing:** Transfer risk to third-party providers
- **Partnerships:** Share risk with business partners

### Risk Avoidance
- **Process Changes:** Change processes to avoid risk
- **Technology Changes:** Change technology to avoid risk
- **Business Changes:** Change business practices to avoid risk
- **Strategic Changes:** Change strategic direction to avoid risk

## Risk Monitoring and Review

### Monitoring Methods
- **Key Risk Indicators:** Quantitative indicators of risk likelihood or impact
- **Risk Dashboards:** Visual representation of risk status and trends
- **Regular Reviews:** Scheduled reviews of risk status and effectiveness
- **Incident Analysis:** Analysis of incidents to identify new risks

### Review Frequency
- **High-Risk Items:** Monthly review
- **Medium-Risk Items:** Quarterly review
- **Low-Risk Items:** Annual review
- **Critical Risks:** Continuous monitoring

### Escalation Procedures
- **Risk Thresholds:** Defined thresholds for risk escalation
- **Escalation Authority:** Authority levels for risk escalation
- **Escalation Timeline:** Timeline for risk escalation
- **Escalation Documentation:** Documentation requirements for risk escalation

## Integration with Governance Framework

### Gate Integration
- **Gate 0:** Risk identification in business justification
- **Gate 1:** Risk assessment in impact analysis
- **Gate 2:** Risk mitigation in security review
- **Gate 3:** Risk acceptance in procurement approval
- **Gate 4:** Risk monitoring in implementation readiness
- **Gate 5:** Risk validation in post-implementation verification

### Risk Management Integration
- **Risk Assessment:** Risk assessment integrated into all gate processes
- **Risk Mitigation:** Risk mitigation integrated into technical and security reviews
- **Risk Monitoring:** Risk monitoring integrated into ongoing operations
- **Risk Reporting:** Risk reporting integrated into governance and compliance processes

### Compliance Integration
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Risk management integrated into regulatory compliance
- **Audit Support:** Risk register supports audit and compliance processes
- **Policy Compliance:** Risk management integrated into policy compliance
- **Incident Response:** Risk management integrated into incident response processes
