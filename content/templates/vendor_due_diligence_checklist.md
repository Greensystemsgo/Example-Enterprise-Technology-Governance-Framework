# Vendor Due Diligence Checklist

## Required Artifacts

### Security Attestations
- **SOC 2 Type II Report:** Current SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent security attestation
- **Alternative Attestations:** ISO 27001, FedRAMP, or other recognized security frameworks
- **Penetration Testing:** Recent penetration testing report (within 12 months)
- **Vulnerability Assessment:** Current vulnerability assessment and remediation status
- **Security Questionnaire:** Completed security questionnaire (SIG-Lite or equivalent)

### Compliance Documentation
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Documentation of compliance with applicable regulations
- **Data Protection:** Data protection and privacy compliance documentation
- **Industry Standards:** Compliance with relevant industry standards and frameworks
- **Audit Reports:** Recent audit reports and findings
- **Certification Status:** Current security certifications and accreditations

### Operational Requirements
- **Breach Notification SLA:** Documented breach notification procedures and timelines
- **Data Location Disclosure:** Clear documentation of data storage and processing locations
- **Data Retention Policies:** Data retention and disposal policies and procedures
- **Business Continuity:** Business continuity and disaster recovery plans
- **Service Level Agreements:** Detailed service level agreements and performance metrics

### Technical Requirements
- **Architecture Documentation:** System architecture and security controls documentation
- **Access Controls:** User access management and authentication procedures
- **Data Encryption:** Data encryption in transit and at rest documentation
- **Monitoring and Logging:** Security monitoring and logging capabilities
- **Incident Response:** Incident response procedures and contact information

## Due Diligence Process

### Initial Assessment
- **Vendor Classification:** Classify vendor based on risk level and data access
- **Documentation Request:** Request required documentation and attestations
- **Timeline Establishment:** Establish due diligence timeline and milestones
- **Responsibility Assignment:** Assign due diligence responsibilities to appropriate teams

### Documentation Review
- **Security Review:** Security team reviews security attestations and controls
- **Compliance Review:** Compliance team reviews regulatory and industry compliance
- **Technical Review:** Technical teams review architecture and operational requirements
- **Legal Review:** Legal team reviews contracts and liability requirements

### Risk Assessment
- **Risk Identification:** Identify potential risks and vulnerabilities
- **Risk Scoring:** Apply risk scoring methodology to assess vendor risk
- **Mitigation Planning:** Develop risk mitigation strategies and controls
- **Acceptance Decision:** Make informed decision on vendor acceptance

### Ongoing Monitoring
- **Regular Reviews:** Conduct regular vendor reviews and assessments
- **Compliance Monitoring:** Monitor ongoing compliance and certification status
- **Incident Tracking:** Track security incidents and response effectiveness
- **Performance Monitoring:** Monitor service level agreement compliance

## Risk Categories

### High-Risk Vendors
- **Criteria:** Access to sensitive data, critical systems, or high-risk services
- **Requirements:** Enhanced due diligence, additional security controls, regular monitoring
- **Approval:** CAB approval required with detailed risk assessment
- **Monitoring:** Quarterly reviews and continuous monitoring

### Medium-Risk Vendors
- **Criteria:** Limited access to sensitive data, standard business services
- **Requirements:** Standard due diligence, basic security controls, periodic monitoring
- **Approval:** Standard approval process with security team validation
- **Monitoring:** Annual reviews and periodic monitoring

### Low-Risk Vendors
- **Criteria:** No access to sensitive data, low-risk services
- **Requirements:** Basic due diligence, minimal security controls, standard monitoring
- **Approval:** Standard approval process with basic validation
- **Monitoring:** Annual reviews and standard monitoring

## Documentation Requirements

### Vendor Profile
- **Company Information:** Company name, address, contact information
- **Service Description:** Detailed description of services provided
- **Data Access:** Description of data access and processing requirements
- **Risk Classification:** Vendor risk classification and justification

### Security Documentation
- **Security Attestations:** All required security attestations and reports
- **Security Controls:** Description of implemented security controls
- **Incident Response:** Incident response procedures and contact information
- **Security Monitoring:** Security monitoring and logging capabilities

### Compliance Documentation
- **Regulatory Compliance:** Compliance with applicable regulations
- **Industry Standards:** Compliance with relevant industry standards
- **Audit Reports:** Recent audit reports and findings
- **Certification Status:** Current certifications and accreditations

### Operational Documentation
- **Service Level Agreements:** Detailed SLAs and performance metrics
- **Business Continuity:** Business continuity and disaster recovery plans
- **Data Management:** Data handling, retention, and disposal procedures
- **Support Procedures:** Support procedures and escalation processes

## Approval Process

### Initial Review
- **Documentation Review:** Review all required documentation and attestations
- **Risk Assessment:** Conduct comprehensive risk assessment
- **Gap Analysis:** Identify gaps and areas of concern
- **Recommendation:** Provide recommendation for vendor acceptance

### CAB Review
- **Risk Presentation:** Present risk assessment and findings to CAB
- **Discussion:** Discuss risks, mitigations, and acceptance criteria
- **Decision:** CAB makes decision on vendor acceptance
- **Documentation:** Document CAB decision and rationale

### Executive Approval
- **High-Risk Vendors:** Executive approval required for high-risk vendors
- **Risk Presentation:** Present risk assessment and CAB recommendation
- **Decision:** Executive makes final decision on vendor acceptance
- **Documentation:** Document executive decision and rationale

## Integration with Governance Framework

### Gate Integration
- **Gate 3:** Vendor due diligence integrated into procurement approval process
- **Documentation:** Due diligence results documented in procurement artifacts
- **Risk Assessment:** Vendor risk assessment integrated into overall risk management
- **Approval:** Vendor approval integrated into CAB decision-making process

### Ongoing Management
- **Regular Reviews:** Regular vendor reviews integrated into governance framework
- **Risk Monitoring:** Vendor risk monitoring integrated into risk management
- **Incident Response:** Vendor incidents integrated into incident response process
- **Compliance Validation:** Vendor compliance integrated into audit and compliance processes
