# Document Hierarchy & Maintenance Standards

## Document Types and Ownership

### Policy
- **Definition:** Executive-level direction and mandatory requirements that establish organizational governance principles
- **Ownership:** Executive leadership and governance bodies
- **Approval Authority:** Executive leadership with stakeholder notification
- **Scope:** Organization-wide mandatory requirements and strategic direction
- **Examples:** Cybersecurity policy, data governance policy, vendor management policy

### Standard
- **Definition:** Technical and operational requirements that define how policies are implemented
- **Ownership:** Subject matter experts and technical teams
- **Approval Authority:** Change Advisory Board (CAB) with technical validation
- **Scope:** Technical implementation requirements and operational procedures
- **Examples:** Security baseline standards, logging requirements, change management standards

### Procedure
- **Definition:** Step-by-step implementation guidance for operational activities
- **Ownership:** Operational teams and process owners
- **Approval Authority:** Team leads with peer review
- **Scope:** Detailed operational procedures and workflows
- **Examples:** Account provisioning procedures, incident response procedures, backup procedures

### Guideline
- **Definition:** Recommended best practices and optional guidance for consistent implementation
- **Ownership:** Teams and subject matter experts
- **Approval Authority:** Peer review and team validation
- **Scope:** Recommended practices and implementation guidance
- **Examples:** Naming conventions, troubleshooting guides, best practice recommendations

## Version and Approval Requirements

### Version Control
- All documents must include version numbers, approval dates, and review schedules
- Version format: Major.Minor.Revision (e.g., 1.0.0, 1.1.0, 1.1.1)
- Change tracking must document what changed, why, and when

### Approval Workflows
- **Policy Changes:** Executive approval required with stakeholder notification and implementation timeline
- **Standard Changes:** CAB review required with technical validation and impact assessment
- **Procedure Changes:** Team lead approval required with peer review and operational validation
- **Guideline Changes:** Peer review required with team feedback and best practice validation

### Document Metadata
- Document title and purpose
- Version number and date
- Approval authority and date
- Review schedule and next review date
- Document owner and contact information
- Related documents and dependencies

## Annual Review Cadence

### Policy Review
- **Frequency:** Annual review with executive oversight
- **Process:** Executive leadership review with stakeholder input and regulatory compliance validation
- **Outcomes:** Updated policies, implementation guidance, and compliance requirements

### Standard Review
- **Frequency:** Annual review with CAB participation
- **Process:** Technical team review with CAB validation and industry best practice assessment
- **Outcomes:** Updated standards, technical requirements, and implementation guidance

### Procedure Review
- **Frequency:** Annual review with operational team validation
- **Process:** Process owner review with team feedback and operational effectiveness assessment
- **Outcomes:** Updated procedures, workflow improvements, and training materials

### Guideline Review
- **Frequency:** Annual review with peer feedback integration
- **Process:** Team review with peer feedback and best practice validation
- **Outcomes:** Updated guidelines, best practice recommendations, and implementation guidance

## Document Lifecycle Management

### Creation
- Identify document type and ownership
- Define scope and approval authority
- Establish review schedule and dependencies
- Create initial version with proper metadata

### Maintenance
- Regular review and updates based on schedule
- Change tracking and version control
- Stakeholder notification for significant changes
- Training and communication for updates

### Retirement
- End-of-life planning and migration
- Archive requirements and retention policies
- Stakeholder notification and transition planning
- Documentation of replacement or successor documents

## Integration with Governance Framework

### Gate Integration
- Document requirements referenced in gate processes
- Approval workflows integrated with CAB oversight
- Version control aligned with change management
- Review schedules coordinated with annual framework refresh

### Compliance and Audit
- Document retention aligned with records management policies
- Audit trail maintenance for all document changes
- Compliance validation with regulatory requirements
- Evidence collection for audit and review processes
